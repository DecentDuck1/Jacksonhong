Chefle

A food guessing game with a new mystery dish each day. You get seven guesses, with clues for protein, carbs, serving temperature, calories, texture, and prep/cook time.

The day’s food region narrows the list of possible dishes. There’s also an infinite mode for playing past the daily puzzle.
